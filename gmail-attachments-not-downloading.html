Gmail attachments not downloading: what to try

You click the attachment and nothing happens, or the download fails halfway. The cause is usually in the browser, not in the email. Work through these fixes, from the quickest to the slowest.

1. Reload Gmail and try again

Refresh the Gmail tab, open the email again and click the attachment once. A stale tab is the most common reason a click does nothing.

2. Check that Chrome may download files

Open Chrome settings, go to Privacy and security, then Site settings, and look for the automatic downloads permission. Make sure mail.google.com is not blocked there. Also check that your download folder still exists.

3. Try a private window

Open Gmail in an Incognito window. Extensions are off there by default, so if the download works, something in your normal window is getting in the way.

4. Turn extensions off one by one

Ad blockers, download managers and security extensions can block Gmail downloads. Disable them one at a time in chrome://extensions and test after each.

5. Check free disk space

A full disk makes downloads fail without a clear message. Free some space and try again.

6. Large files arrive as Drive links

Gmail attaches files up to 25 MB. Bigger ones are sent as a Google Drive link inside the message, so there is no attachment to download. Open the link and download from Drive.

7. Look for a security warning

Gmail can warn about or block files it considers unsafe. If you see a warning, do not download the file until you trust the sender.

8. Update Chrome

Open chrome://settings/help and install any pending update, then restart the browser.

When you need many files at once

If the problem is that you have dozens of attachments to save, not that one fails, use InboxZip: open Gmail, press Download ZIP and get all attachments of the emails on the page in one archive. If an export fails, InboxZip says so and saves nothing instead of a broken ZIP.

Questions

Why will my Gmail attachment not download?
Most often Chrome blocks the download, an extension interferes or the file is a Google Drive link. Try a private window first.

Can I download many Gmail attachments at once?
Yes. InboxZip collects the attachments of the emails on the Gmail page into one ZIP.
