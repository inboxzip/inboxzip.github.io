Chrome extension for Gmail

Download all Gmail attachments as one ZIP.

InboxZip collects the attachments from your Gmail search results into a single flat ZIP file, so you don’t have to open every email. It runs inside your browser: your emails and files never pass through our servers.

Add to Chrome Free

How it works

  1. Search in Gmail, for example has:attachment from:accounting.
  2. Open InboxZip next to Gmail and choose the file types you want: PDF, images or other.
  3. Press Download ZIP. InboxZip fetches the attachments with your own Gmail session and builds the archive on your computer.

Free

Every attachment on the Gmail results page you are looking at, up to 3 exports per day (failed or cancelled exports don’t count).

Good to know

  • InboxZip saves files attached to emails (the ones Gmail marks with a paperclip). Images embedded in a message body and calendar invites are not included.
  • One file up to 25 MB; one ZIP up to 100 MB, 1,500 files and 100 result pages.
  • Files with the same name get numbers, so nothing is overwritten. If an export fails, InboxZip says so and saves nothing instead of a broken archive.
  • No account, no Gmail API and no sign-in with Google. Details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

How do I download all attachments from a Gmail search at once?
Search in Gmail, open InboxZip and press Download ZIP. InboxZip saves every attachment on the results page you are looking at into one ZIP.

Can I download only the PDFs from Gmail?
Yes. Pick PDFs, images or other files before you start, and the archive only holds those.

Does InboxZip upload my emails or attachments?
No. The files go from Gmail to your browser and the ZIP is built on your computer.

What if two attachments have the same name?
Both are kept: the second one gets a number, so nothing is overwritten.
